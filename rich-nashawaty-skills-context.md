## Measurement Discipline (Differentiator)
Built a repeatable measurement methodology adapted from quantitative analysis:
1. **Fetch** data from APIs on a schedule
2. **Rate / model** performance units
3. **Calibrate** — verify stated confidence matches reality
4. **Benchmark** — compare against market/baseline to find signal vs. noise
5. **Forward-test** — log predictions, grade against actual outcomes over time
6. **Automate** — the loop runs itself, accumulates honest ledger

Applied this to SEO via **holdout analysis** — comparing treated pages (received SEO work) vs. untouched control pages to prove causal impact rather than correlation. Almost no local SEO consultants do this.

**Differentiator statement:** "The only local SEO consultant who proves whether my work actually moved the needle — using holdout and calibration discipline applied to your specific business data."
