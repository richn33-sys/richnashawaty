What's included

Four dimensions. One clear picture.

For a small business, four dimensions determine whether you're ready to automate — and what to do first.

📋
Processes & Data Audit
Which workflows exist, how consistent they are, where data lives and whether it's in a usable format. This is where most small businesses find their biggest gaps — and their quickest wins. Automation runs on defined inputs. If those don't exist, this section tells you exactly what to build first.
🔧
Technology Stack Audit
A review of every tool you're currently paying for — CRM, project management, communication, marketing — with an analysis of unused automation capabilities. Most small businesses find they already have the infrastructure; they just haven't activated it.

Team Capacity Assessment
Who on your team could own and maintain automations, where bandwidth currently sits, and how to sequence AI adoption without overwhelming staff. The most technically correct automation still fails if nobody owns it after launch.

Governance Framework
Who decides what gets automated, who reviews AI-generated outputs before they reach customers, and what the escalation path looks like when something breaks. Even a one-person business needs a simple governance model — this section defines it for your situation.

A prioritized list of automation opportunities with estimated time, cost, and ROI for each — sequenced to deliver quick wins first, then build toward more complex automations. A document you can hand to a developer or consultant to execute immediately.

Four dimensions: your data and processes (what workflows exist, how consistent they are, what data they depend on), your technology stack (tools you're paying for and whether automation capabilities are being used), your team capacity (who can own automations after they're built), and governance (how AI use decisions get made and who reviews outputs).
