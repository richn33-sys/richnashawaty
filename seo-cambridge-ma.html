Cambridge search behavior

Cambridge isn't Boston. The search behavior is different.

Cambridge residents search with neighborhood precision. A Kendall Square employee doesn't search "restaurant Boston" — they search "lunch near Kendall/MIT" or "coffee near Draper." Businesses that optimize for "Cambridge MA" and nothing else miss most of the actual searches.

Innovation district
Kendall Square
The highest-density tech and biotech corridor in the country. Employees, researchers, and startup founders searching for B2B services, professional development, and local amenities during the workday.
Common modifiers: "near Kendall" · "near MIT" · "near Kendall/MIT T"
Academic + retail
Harvard Square
High foot traffic, strong local retail competition, significant tourist and student search volume. Local businesses here compete against national brands for "near Harvard" and "Harvard Square" queries.
Common modifiers: "near Harvard" · "Harvard Square" · "near Harvard T"
Diverse · residential
Central & Inman Square
Dense residential neighborhoods with strong community identity. Residents search hyperlocally — "Central Square dentist" or "Inman Square plumber" rather than "Cambridge" broadly.
Common modifiers: "Central Square" · "Inman Square" · "near Central T"
