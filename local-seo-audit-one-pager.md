## What this is

A fixed-scope audit of your Google Business Profile and local search presence, benchmarked directly against your three closest competitors. You get a clear picture of exactly where they're beating you in local search — and a prioritized action list to close the gap.

No vague "improve your SEO" advice. Every finding comes with a specific fix, ranked by impact.

## What you get

**Google Business Profile competitive teardown**
- Category analysis vs. your top 3 competitors — the missing categories that are making you invisible for high-intent searches
- Attribute gap analysis — the profile tags competitors have that you don't
- Review velocity report — how fast competitors earn reviews vs. you, and what customers actually say
- Services, description, and photo audit — your fully-controlled profile content, optimized

**Citation / NAP consistency audit**
- Your business checked across 15+ major directories (Google, Yelp, Bing, Apple Maps, Facebook, BBB, Angi, and more)
- Every inconsistency in your name, address, and phone flagged — these silently suppress your rankings
- Priority fix list plus the high-value directories you're missing entirely

**Deliverables**
- Competitor comparison spreadsheets (the side-by-side that shows exactly where you stand)
- A prioritized action list, ranked high / medium / low impact
- A 60-minute walkthrough call to review findings and next steps
