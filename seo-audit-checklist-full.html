2026 Technical SEO Audit Checklist for Boston Small Businesses

65 checklist items across 7 categories — built from 20 years of SEO audits. Updated for current Core Web Vitals thresholds, AI visibility signals, and Boston-specific local SEO requirements.

65 items 7 categories Updated May 2026 Interactive — click to check off

How to use this checklist

Work through each category in order — Technical Foundation first, since everything else depends on it. Priority labels (HIGH / MED / LOW) indicate what to fix first when resources are limited. Click any item to check it off — your progress is saved in your browser.

01 Technical Foundation 12 items
Site is accessible via HTTPS on all pages
HTTP pages should 301 redirect to HTTPS equivalent
HIGH
No pages blocked by robots.txt that should be indexed
Check yourdomain.com/robots.txt — Disallow: / blocks everything
HIGH
No noindex tags on pages that should appear in search
Check source for: meta name="robots" content="noindex"
HIGH
XML sitemap exists and is submitted to Google Search Console
Sitemap should be at /sitemap.xml and listed in robots.txt
HIGH
Sitemap only includes canonical, indexable URLs
No noindex pages, no redirected URLs, no duplicate versions
MED
www and non-www versions resolve to single canonical version
Pick one and 301 redirect the other — don't serve both
HIGH
index.html redirects to root / (no duplicate homepage)
yourdomain.com/index.html should 301 to yourdomain.com/
MED
No broken internal links (404 errors)
Use Screaming Frog or GSC Coverage report to identify
MED
Redirect chains are minimal (A→B→C should be A→C)
Each redirect hop adds latency and loses some link equity
LOW
Mobile-friendly — passes Google Mobile-Friendly Test
Test at search.google.com/test/mobile-friendly
HIGH
Google Search Console set up and verified
Essential for indexation monitoring, crawl errors, and AI Overview data
HIGH
Google Analytics 4 installed on all pages
Verify via GA4 Realtime report or browser inspector
HIGH
02 Core Web Vitals (2026 Thresholds) 8 items
LCP (Largest Contentful Paint) under 2.5 seconds
Test at pagespeed.web.dev — check both mobile and desktop
HIGH
INP (Interaction to Next Paint) under 200ms
Replaced FID in 2024 — measures responsiveness to user interactions
HIGH
CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift) under 0.1
Layout shift caused by fonts loading is a common culprit — use font-display: swap
HIGH
All three CWV metrics pass (holistic scoring as of 2026)
May 2026 update introduced holistic scoring — failing one impacts overall score even if others pass
HIGH
Google Fonts loaded asynchronously (media="print" pattern)
Prevents render-blocking that inflates LCP and causes CLS
MED
Images have explicit width and height attributes
Prevents layout shift when images load — major CLS contributor
MED
No render-blocking JavaScript on critical path
Defer or async all non-critical JS — check PageSpeed Insights recommendations
MED
CWV passing on mobile (not just desktop)
Google primarily uses mobile scores — desktop passing is not sufficient
HIGH
03 On-Page SEO 11 items
Every page has a unique, descriptive title tag under 60 characters
Lead with primary keyword — avoid brand name at front unless it's well known
HIGH
Every page has a unique meta description under 155 characters
Write for CTR — this is your ad copy in search results
HIGH
Each page has exactly one H1 tag
H1 should match or closely reflect the page's target keyword
HIGH
H2/H3 hierarchy is logical and keyword-rich
Headers should outline the page like a document — not decorative labels
MED
Every page has a canonical tag pointing to its preferred URL
Prevents duplicate content issues — especially important for paginated or filtered pages
MED
Images have descriptive alt text
Describe what's in the image — not keyword stuffing, actual description
MED
Internal linking connects related pages naturally
Anchor text should be descriptive — not "click here" or "read more"
MED
Service pages have sufficient content depth (500+ words)
Thin service pages rarely rank in competitive markets — add specifics, outcomes, FAQs
HIGH
Content demonstrates first-hand expertise (E-E-A-T)
Specific examples, real outcomes, genuine opinions — not generic information anyone could write
HIGH
Author bylines on blog content with credentials
Link author name to an about page with verifiable credentials
MED
Publication and last-updated dates visible on time-sensitive content
Signals freshness — especially important for posts about current topics
LOW
04 Local SEO Signals (Boston-Specific) 10 items
Google Business Profile claimed, verified, and complete
The single highest-impact local SEO action — free and essential for map pack visibility
HIGH
GBP business name, address, phone (NAP) exactly matches website
Inconsistencies between GBP and site undermine local ranking signals
HIGH
GBP categories accurate — primary and secondary
Primary category is the most important ranking signal in GBP
HIGH
GBP has photos — at least cover image and profile photo
Profiles with photos get significantly more clicks in map results
MED
Boston/neighborhood mentioned in H1, title tag, and body copy
Geo-signals in content reinforce local relevance — be specific (Back Bay, Cambridge, etc.)
HIGH
NAP consistent across all major directories (Yelp, BBB, Clutch)
Citation inconsistency is one of the most common local SEO problems
MED
Listed in Boston-specific directories (Boston Chamber, BBB Boston)
High-authority local citations with strong geo-signal value
MED
At least 5 genuine reviews on GBP
Reviews are the #1 local ranking factor after proximity — prioritize getting early reviews
HIGH
LocalBusiness schema on homepage with accurate address and service area
Reinforces local signals for both Google and AI search tools
MED
Service area pages or neighborhood-specific content where relevant
Boston searches are hyperlocal — neighborhood-specific content captures intent others miss
LOW
05 Schema & Structured Data 9 items
LocalBusiness schema on homepage
Include name, address, phone, url, description, and areaServed
HIGH
Person schema on about page with credentials and alumniOf
Verifiable author identity is an E-E-A-T signal AI systems reference
MED
FAQPage schema on service pages
FAQ schema gets cited frequently in AI Overviews — direct, specific answers
HIGH
Article schema on blog posts with author, datePublished, dateModified
Enables rich results and signals freshness to both traditional and AI search
MED
BreadcrumbList schema on blog posts and inner pages
Helps AI understand site structure and can appear in search result snippets
MED
Schema validated with Google's Rich Results Test
Test at search.google.com/test/rich-results — fix any errors before deploying
MED
Open Graph tags on all pages (og:title, og:description, og:type)
Controls how pages appear when shared on social media and messaging apps
LOW
Service schema on service pages with provider and areaServed
Reinforces what you offer and where — useful for AI citation of service businesses
MED
No schema errors in Google Search Console Enhancements report
GSC flags schema issues that prevent rich results — check regularly
MED
06 AI Visibility Signals 8 items
robots.txt explicitly allows major AI crawlers
Add: User-agent: GPTBot, User-agent: ClaudeBot, User-agent: PerplexityBot — all with Allow: /
HIGH
FAQ sections on key pages with direct, specific answers
AI systems cite FAQ content frequently — direct answers to real questions your customers ask
HIGH
Author identity clearly verifiable (about page + bylines + schema)
AI systems weight verifiable author credentials when selecting content to cite
HIGH
Content demonstrates first-hand expertise — not just summarized information
The test: could this have been written by someone who has never done this work? If yes — add expert perspective
HIGH
GSC AI Overview impressions being tracked
GSC Performance report now shows AI Overview impression data — monitor monthly
MED
Branded search volume tracked as AI visibility indicator
Rising branded searches correlate with AI mentions — track in GSC Queries filtered by brand
LOW
Manual spot checks for key queries in Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity
Search your top 5 target queries in incognito — are you being cited? What content is referenced?
MED
No lapsed or spammy tactics targeting AI specifically (llms.txt, prompt injection)
Google has confirmed these have no positive impact — wasted effort vs. content quality
LOW
07 Authority & Links 7 items
No manual actions or penalties in Google Search Console
GSC Security & Manual Actions — any issues here override everything else
HIGH
No obviously toxic or spammy backlinks
Check Ahrefs or GSC Links report — disavow genuinely toxic links if present
MED
At least a few quality backlinks from relevant local or industry sources
Local directories, partner sites, industry associations — quality over quantity
MED
No significant duplicate content across pages
Identical or near-identical service pages dilute ranking signals — consolidate or differentiate
MED
Outbound links to authoritative sources where relevant
Linking to credible sources signals topical awareness — don't link out excessively but don't avoid it
LOW
External links open in same tab (not forced _blank) unless truly appropriate
Unnecessary _blank links can hurt engagement signals — only use for documents or truly external resources
LOW
Brand mentions monitored — unclaimed mentions converted to links where possible
Sites that mention your brand without linking are link reclamation opportunities
LOW
