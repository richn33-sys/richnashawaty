Common questions

FAQ

What tools do you use to build automations? +

Primarily n8n and Zapier, depending on complexity and your existing stack. For AI-powered workflows, I use the Claude API and OpenAI where appropriate. All tools are ones your team can maintain after handoff — no proprietary platforms that create dependency.

Do I need technical knowledge to work with you? +

No. You need to know your business processes — I handle the technical implementation. The documentation I deliver is written for non-technical users so you can understand and maintain what was built without needing a developer.

How long does a typical build take? +

Single workflow builds typically take 1–2 weeks from project kickoff to deployment. Multi-workflow builds take 2–4 weeks. Timeline depends on complexity, integration count, and how quickly you can provide access to the tools and data needed.

What if the automation breaks after you hand it off? +

All builds include a support window (30 days for single workflows, 60 days for multi-workflow builds) during which I fix any issues at no additional cost. After that, ongoing maintenance is available on a retainer or per-incident basis. The documentation also walks through the most common failure points and how to address them.

Do you combine AI consulting with SEO? +

Yes — and that combination is increasingly valuable. AI automation can handle content research, brief generation, publishing workflows, and reporting for SEO programs. Businesses that combine both services get compounding returns: better search visibility driving more leads, with automation handling the operational overhead of running the program.
