Common questions
What is an AI readiness assessment?
It's a quick diagnostic that tells you whether your business is set up to get real value from AI automation — looking at practical factors like process consistency, your data, ownership, goal clarity, and budget, then giving you a score and specific next steps. This free version takes about two minutes and shows an instant on-page result.
Do I need to know anything technical to take it?
No. Every question is written in plain English for business owners. You don't need to know what an API, an integration, or a large language model is — the questions are about how your business actually runs day to day.

No. Your score, tier, and next steps appear instantly on the page — nothing is hidden behind a form. After you see your result, you can optionally have the assessment and a personalized action plan emailed to you.
