Background
"Most businesses can't afford a Director of SEO. Most SEO freelancers have never worked at that level. I've done both."

I started in SEO before most people knew what it was. In 2006 I took on my first client under White Mountain Process — a manufacturing company with no online presence — and built their digital revenue from zero to $750k using search as the primary channel. That experience of doing real work for real results never left me.

From there I moved into agencies, managing SEO across dozens of accounts and learning what works at scale. Then enterprise: Kayak, Monster, Ziff Davis, Care.com. At Ziff Davis I was Director of SEO — overseeing strategy across a multi-brand media portfolio. At Monster I managed SEO in 19 languages across 11 global domains.

Now I work directly with small businesses in Boston and Greater New England. The same strategic thinking that drove results at global scale, applied to businesses that actually need it — and can move fast enough to use it.
