How to use it

  1. 1 Start with what should show up. Four things: a technical change log, the content that published, a performance summary tied to a business outcome, and next month's plan. Check the last report you received against all four. What's missing is the conversation to have.
  2. 2 Run the real-vs-filler comparison. Technical, content, performance — each shown both ways. The test is whether a deliverable is specific (names things), narrated (a person explains it), and honest (admits what didn't move). Charts aren't the problem; charts instead of a narrative are.
  3. 3 Scan the red flags. Five of them. One is worth a question. Several, month after month, is worth a straight conversation — even if the underlying work turns out to be fine, not knowing is a problem on its own.
