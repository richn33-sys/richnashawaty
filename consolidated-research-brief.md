## P1 — Highest priority (do first)

**1. Blog post: "SEO Consultant vs. SEO Agency: Which Should Your Boston Small Business Hire?"**
Target: `seo consultant vs agency`. **Consensus: 4/4, all HIGH.**
Pure bottom-funnel — buyer is ready to hire and deciding *who*. Owns your single biggest differentiator (senior solo, no handoffs, no agency layers). Angle: ideal for $1M–$10M Boston businesses wanting direct expert access. Currently deferred — this is the un-defer candidate.

**2. GBP cluster — one push, three assets (pillar + service + lead magnet)** — Consensus: 3/4, mostly HIGH.
A complete local funnel; build together:
- **Pillar post:** "Google Business Profile Optimization for Boston Businesses: The 2026 Checklist" — target `google business profile optimization boston`. Angle: neighborhood + MBTA T-stop targeting, review velocity (50+ reviews @ 4.5+ for Local Pack), weekly-posting cadence, AI-readiness for Maps summaries. Primary category = biggest local ranking lever.
- **Productized service:** standalone **GBP Audit + Optimization, $297–$497 one-time.** Low-commitment entry point most consultants don't offer (they bundle GBP into retainers). Natural step-up to your $497 Local SEO Audit.
- **Lead magnet:** "Boston GBP Optimization Checklist" (PDF) — free self-audit; the Boston-specific angles (MBTA, neighborhood) aren't packaged by any competitor.

**3. AI Readiness Assessment — post + interactive tool** — Consensus: 2/4, HIGH.
- **Tool/lead magnet:** 5–7 question interactive scorecard (same pattern as your AI Automation ROI Calculator). Qualified lead-gen — captures prospects who already know they have workflow gaps, ahead of the AI Consulting page. (Only ~9% of SMBs have fully embedded AI; the rest need a starting point.)
- **Supporting post:** "AI Readiness Assessment for Small Business: Is Your Boston Company Ready to Automate?" — target `ai readiness assessment small business`.
