About Rich
"I've done SEO at every level — freelance, agency, and enterprise Director. Now I work directly with businesses that deserve that experience."

I started doing SEO independently around 2006 — before most companies even had a dedicated SEO strategy. I built an online presence from nothing for a manufacturing company and grew it to $750k in annual revenue using SEO, paid search, and content. That early grounding in what actually drives business results shaped everything that came after.

From there I moved into agencies across the Boston area — working across dozens of client accounts and learning what scales across industries. Then in-house at increasingly complex organizations: travel, media, recruiting — eventually leading SEO strategy across global properties with millions of pages, 19 languages, and 11 international domains.

That full arc is the differentiator. I've been the solo operator, the agency strategist, and the enterprise Director. I understand budget constraints, engineering bottlenecks, content team dynamics, and executive reporting because I've lived all of it — not just read about it.

More recently I've built real AI automation systems for myself — trading bots, content pipelines, passive income sites. I bring that same hands-on mindset to every client engagement.
