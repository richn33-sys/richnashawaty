Do I need to write the content? +

No. I write the content based on a short conversation about your business. You review it and tell me what to change. Most clients are surprised how little input is needed — I've done this enough to know what works for local service businesses.

What do I need to provide? +

Your business name, phone number, service area, list of services, and any photos you have (even phone photos are fine). If you have Google reviews you're proud of, point me to your GBP listing. That's it.

Yes — the site is built with local SEO fundamentals in place from day one. It won't rank overnight, but it will be indexed by Google within a few days of launch and will start building visibility from there. Linking it to your Google Business Profile (which I'll help you do) accelerates that.

Same process, same price. A lot of businesses have websites that are outdated, slow, or hard to use on mobile. If yours isn't working for you, a clean rebuild at $500 is almost always the right call over trying to fix something that's fundamentally broken.
