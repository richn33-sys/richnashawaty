Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rich Nashawaty ("I", "me", or "my") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit richnashawaty.com or contact me about services.

I take your privacy seriously and keep data collection to the absolute minimum needed to communicate with you and improve the site.

Information I Collect

Information you provide directly: When you contact me via email, a contact form, or a booking link, I receive your name, email address, and any information you choose to share about your business or project. I use this only to respond to your inquiry and conduct any work we agree to.

Information collected automatically: Like most websites, this site may collect basic technical information when you visit — such as your browser type, pages viewed, and approximate location (country/region level). This is collected through standard web hosting logs and any analytics tools in use.

How I Use Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries and schedule strategy calls
  • To deliver consulting services and project work you've engaged me for
  • To send follow-up communications related to our work together
  • To understand how the site is being used and improve it over time

I do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Ever.

Email Communications

If you contact me or sign up for any updates, your email address is used only to communicate with you directly. You can opt out of non-essential communications at any time by replying to any email or contacting me at contact@richnashawaty.com.

Cookies

This site may use cookies for basic functionality and analytics. These are standard, non-invasive cookies that do not track you across other websites. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time without affecting your ability to use the site.

Third-Party Services

This site may use third-party tools including:

  • Google Analytics — for anonymized site traffic analysis
  • Calendly — for scheduling strategy calls (subject to Calendly's own privacy policy)
  • Google Fonts — for typography (subject to Google's privacy policy)

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. I choose tools that take privacy seriously and configure them to minimize data collection where possible.

Data Retention

I retain contact information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was provided — typically for the duration of our working relationship and a reasonable period afterward for follow-up. You can request deletion of your information at any time.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal data I hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt out of any non-essential communications

To exercise any of these rights, contact me at contact@richnashawaty.com and I'll respond promptly.

Security

I take reasonable steps to protect any information you share with me. The site uses HTTPS encryption. Email communications are handled through secure providers. That said, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure — please don't send sensitive financial or personal information via email.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, I'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or how your data is handled? Reach me at contact@richnashawaty.com.
