What GEO consulting actually involves.

01
AI Visibility Audit
Where does your business currently appear in AI-generated answers? Which competitors are being cited and why? An audit maps your current AI footprint and identifies the specific gaps — content, structure, authority — holding you back from citation.
02
Answer-First Content Strategy
AI systems cite content that answers questions directly in the first sentence, not content that buries the answer three paragraphs in. Rewriting your service pages and blog posts to answer-first structure is the highest-impact GEO tactic for most businesses.
03
E-E-A-T & Authority Signals
AI systems favor content with verifiable expertise. Author credentials, business history, named sources, and third-party citations all signal trustworthiness. I audit and strengthen these signals across your site and digital presence.
04
Schema & Structured Data
FAQPage, LocalBusiness, Article, and BreadcrumbList schema give AI systems a machine-readable understanding of your content. Proper structured data implementation is a direct path to AI Overview eligibility for relevant queries.
05
Google Business Profile Optimization
For local queries, GBP data is one of the primary signals Google uses to populate AI Overviews. A complete, accurate, actively managed GBP — with correct categories, services, and reviews — is the most direct path to local AI visibility.

Common questions

FAQ

How do I know if AI Overviews are appearing for my category? +

Search your main service keywords in Google — if you see an AI-generated answer block above the organic results, AI Overviews are active for your category. You can also check Google Search Console: if your property is getting AI Overview impressions, they'll show up in the Search Appearance filter in the Performance report.

Can I get cited in AI Overviews even if I don't rank #1 organically? +

Yes — this is one of the most important things to understand about GEO. AI systems don't simply cite the #1 ranked page. They cite the page that answers the specific question most directly and credibly. A page at position 7 that opens with a direct, expert answer can be cited over a page at position 1 that buries the answer. This is why answer-first content structure is the highest-leverage GEO tactic for most businesses.

What is AI visibility and why does it matter for my business? +

AI visibility is whether your business appears in AI-generated answers across Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and similar platforms. These systems increasingly serve as the first answer people see before they click a blue link. A citation in an AI Overview generates brand awareness even without a click. As AI search becomes more prevalent, businesses with established AI visibility will have a compounding advantage over those that haven't invested in it yet.

How long does it take to see GEO results? +

GBP-related improvements to local AI visibility can show within 2–4 weeks. Content and schema changes take 4–8 weeks to be re-crawled and reflected in AI system outputs. Building the E-E-A-T and authority signals that AI systems favor takes longer — 3–6 months — but those signals compound over time.
